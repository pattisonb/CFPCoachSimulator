# Coach Career mode — heuristic, anti-pattern and WCAG 2.2 evaluation

Evaluator: Vigil (Intent). Date: 2026-10-27.

## Method and scope

Evaluated 27 screens of Coach Career mode from full-page screenshots at 1280px (dark and light) and 400px (dark and light), the visible-text captures, and the automated checks (`checks_summary.txt` / `checks.json`). Every screen was inspected at 1280-dark and 400-light; 1280-light and 400-dark were used where contrast or theme mattered (depth chart, recruiting side panel, Keep them home, Staff hire). Read-only source checks were made in `web/season.js`, `web/recruiting.js`, `web/offseason.js` and `web/styles.css` only to confirm how specific controls behave.

Not evaluated: loading and network-failure states (none captured), actual screen-reader output, keyboard focus order and focus visibility (not observable from static captures), motion. Findings about keyboard and assistive technology are inferred from markup and visible text and are flagged as such.

Table-header case: the DOM has mixed-case headers (`Program`, `Week`, `Offense`) but the CSS renders them uppercase on the programs, schedule, depth-chart and season-by-season tables. That is a consistent house convention and is not reported as an inconsistency. The one table that renders sentence case (Coaches for hire) is reported (F28).

## UX health score: 68 / 100

| Component | Weight | Score | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task completion (recruit, set depth chart, run offseason) | 30 | 22 | Every core task completes on desktop; on phone the depth chart is hard to operate and the recruiting pitch panel is far off-screen |
| Heuristic conformance (H1–H10) | 30 | 20 | Strong help text and empty states; consistency and minimalism are the weak heuristics |
| Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) | 25 | 15 | One clear AA failure (depth-chart controls); several small-target and hover-only patterns; themes otherwise contrast well |
| Robustness (edge cases, overflow, data) | 15 | 11 | Literal `null` rendered, clipped button label, three tables wider than a phone viewport |

A 68 means: a capable, well-explained product with one accessibility failure and a cluster of polish defects, most of them one-line fixes.

## Anti-pattern verdict: Clean

No confirmshaming, no fabricated urgency, no pre-checked manipulation, no hidden costs. Every action that spends a resource (hours, NIL, offers) shows its cost on the control ("Call 15h", "Offer NIL from next year's"). The lowball warning on the Offer panel and the "a weak case ... backfires" note are honest pre-disclosure, not manipulation. "Sim to next season" sits beside every stage CTA; it is a legitimate shortcut, not a trick, but see F23 for the confirmation question.

## Findings

Tags: SMALL = local styling, wording, hidden-state or wrap change. LARGE = flow, layout or structural change.

### P0 — blocks a core task or violates accessibility law

**F1. Depth-chart move controls are below minimum target size, hidden at 35% opacity, and hover-revealed.**
Screen: depth, player-week0, player-history (the depth chart behind the modal). Element: `.depth-ctl button.tiny` with text `★`, `▲`, `▼`. Problem: `button.tiny` is `line-height: 18px; min-height: 0; padding: 0 5px`, measured 23×20px; `.depth-table td.depth-cell .depth-ctl { opacity: 0.35 }` with `opacity: 1` only on `td:hover`; the buttons are icon-only with a `title` but no `aria-label`. On a phone there is no hover, so the controls stay at 35% for the whole session (visible in depth-400-light as faint grey glyphs). Why it matters: setting the depth chart is a core task; the controls fail WCAG 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum, 24×24), 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (a 35%-opacity grey glyph on either theme is well under 3:1), and 1.4.13 Content on Hover is not satisfied because the hover is the only way to reach full visibility. `title` is read by most screen readers but is not a reliable accessible name. Heuristic/WCAG: H6, WCAG 2.5.8, 1.4.11, 1.4.13, 4.1.2. Fix: give the buttons `min-width: 28px; min-height: 28px`, drop the 0.35 opacity (or only reduce it on pointer:fine devices and never below 3:1), and add `aria-label="Make starter"` / `"Move up"` / `"Move down"` alongside the existing `title`. SMALL.

### P1 — major friction

**F2. "Promise a start" button label is clipped to "romise a star".**
Screen: offseason-keep, offseason-keep-met, at 1280 in both themes. Element: the green button `Promise a start / he would start` in each departing-player card. Problem: the three-button row (`Meet`, `Promise a start`, `Let him go`) does not fit the card width and the middle button's label is cut on both sides. Why it matters: this is one of the three decisions the player makes about every departing starter; an unreadable label on the highlighted (green) option defeats the recommendation it is trying to make. Heuristic/WCAG: H6, H4; WCAG 1.4.10 Reflow in spirit (content lost at the default width). Fix: let the row wrap (`flex-wrap: wrap`) or set the button to `white-space: normal; min-width: 0` so the label breaks onto two lines like it does at 400px. SMALL.

**F3. The scouted prospect profile prints the word "null".**
Screen: prospect-profile-scouted (both widths, both themes). Element: the line directly under the `SCOUTED Potential A-–B+ …` strip, reading `null`. Problem: `recruiting.js:711-712` appends `scoutLine(...)` and a ternary that evaluates to `null` when there is no "our comp" to show; `node.append(null)` coerces to the text node "null". Why it matters: a raw programming artefact on the screen where the player is deciding whether to spend hours and an offer undermines trust in every grade next to it. Heuristic/WCAG: H2, H1. Fix: filter nulls before appending (`[a, b].filter(Boolean)`), or only append the comp paragraph when it exists. SMALL.

**F4. Numbers shown out of 100 against the owner's rule.**
Screens and elements: office "YOUR AD'S GOAL" chip `SECURE · 70/100`; every offseason stage, "YOUR MEETING WITH THE AD" tile `JOB SECURITY 100 / 100`; offseason-staff "YOUR JOB" sentence `Job security 100 of 100 (patience: 2 years)`. Problem: the rest of the product deliberately uses word scales (Fair / Solid / Strong / Elite, Weak, Cold / Hot) and relative bars; these three places leak a program-style 0–100 number. Why it matters: it contradicts the house vocabulary (the owner's explicit rule) and invites the player to game a number rather than read a state. Heuristic/WCAG: H4, H2. Fix: show the word already in the chip (`SECURE`, `ON THE HOT SEAT`, etc.) with the existing bar, and change the sentence to "Job security: secure (patience: 2 years)". SMALL.

**F5. On a phone, selecting a prospect card gives no visible feedback and the pitch/offer panel is 50 cards away.**
Screen: recruiting-board-400, recruiting-side-panel-400, offseason-portal-w1-400. Element: `SELECTED · TREY SPENCER` panel (`.go-board` side column) which stacks after the last card at 400px; on desktop with 50 cards (recruiting-board-1280) the panel sits at the top-right and scrolls out of view. Problem: tapping a card highlights its border but the thing that changed (the pitch and NIL offer form) is thousands of pixels below. Why it matters: the pitch is where the recruiting decision is made; on phone it is effectively hidden, and on desktop a long board loses it. Heuristic/WCAG: H1, H6. Fix: on narrow widths render the selected panel as a bottom sheet or inline directly under the tapped card; on desktop make the panel `position: sticky; top`. LARGE.

**F6. Transfer-portal cards show five hollow stars, which reads as a zero-star prospect.**
Screen: offseason-portal-w1 (all widths). Element: `☆☆☆☆☆` under each name (`.go-stars`). Problem: the prospects list itself says "no stars on a transfer: his film is his rating", yet the board renders the empty star row. Why it matters: a blank five-star scale is a rating ("he is a 0"), not an absence of one; it misleads the comparison between transfers and high-schoolers that the player is making in the same week. Heuristic/WCAG: H2, H8. Fix: omit the star row for `b.portal` cards (or show the `TRANSFER` tag in its place). SMALL.

### P2 — minor, recoverable

**F7. Disabled controls use `cursor: wait`.**
Screens: prospect-profile-scouted (`Scouted three times: his area scout keeps looking`, op 0.5, cursor wait), offseason-roster-moves (current-position chip `CB 69`, op 0.5, cursor wait). Problem: a wait cursor says "loading", not "unavailable". Heuristic/WCAG: H1, H4. Fix: `cursor: not-allowed` (or `default`) for disabled; reserve `wait` for in-flight requests. SMALL.

**F8. Actions that cost hours are styled as inline underlined links inside a sentence.**
Screen: prospect-profile. Element: `Send a scout (5 hours, look 1 of 3) →` and `Assign your staff →` (246×20 and 132×20) in the "OUR STAFF'S GRADES" paragraph. Problem: a 20px-tall link in running text for an action that spends 5 of 55 weekly hours. Heuristic/WCAG: H4, H5; WCAG 2.5.8 (the inline-text exception technically applies, which is why this is P2). Fix: render them as the house `go-act` buttons on their own row under the paragraph. SMALL.

**F9. Depth chart on a phone needs an undiscoverable horizontal scroll to reach 3rd–5th string.**
Screen: depth-400, player-history-400. Element: `.depth-table` inside `.tab-body { overflow-x: auto }`. Problem: only STARTER and 2ND are visible; the 3RD–5TH columns (and the `★` that promotes a third-stringer) are off-screen with no scroll affordance. Heuristic/WCAG: H1, H7. Fix: at ≤700px stack each position as a card listing its players vertically, or add a visible "scroll for 3rd–5th" shadow/fade. LARGE.

**F10. Programs table, Coaches-for-hire table and Season-by-season stats are wider than the phone viewport.**
Screens: office-programs-400 (`table.stats.go-programs` 851px), offseason-staff-400 / offseason-staff-open-400 (`.off-table` 490px), player-history-400 (`table.stats` Rushing 482px). Problem: each sits in an overflow container, so the page does not break, but the right-hand columns (`THE AD EXPECTS`, `Last job`, `LONG`, `FUM`) are out of reach without a horizontal drag. The schedule table already solves this correctly by dropping RANK and WHERE at 400. Heuristic/WCAG: H4, H7. Fix: apply the schedule's pattern — drop or stack secondary columns under 700px. LARGE.

**F11. "Last job" column repeats the "Why he is available" text when there is no last job.**
Screen: offseason-staff, offseason-staff-open. Element: `COACHES FOR HIRE` table rows such as `Drake Griffin, 56 | head coach | 76 | Ready for a bigger job | Ready for a bigger job`. Problem: a data fallback is printing the reason into the job column. Heuristic/WCAG: H2, H8. Fix: show "—" or "lower division / NFL" when `last_job` is empty. SMALL.

**F12. The candidate "HIRE" control reads as a disabled bar.**
Screen: offseason-staff-open (both themes). Element: full-width dark/grey bar with small muted `HIRE` text at the foot of each candidate card. Problem: the house primary is a red filled button; this one is grey-on-grey, low contrast in light theme, and looks inert. Heuristic/WCAG: H4, H6; WCAG 1.4.11 (borderline). Fix: use the `go-act` / primary button style with "Hire Arthur Smith". SMALL.

**F13. Scouted grade strip uses an ambiguous dash between letter grades.**
Screen: prospect-profile-scouted, recruiting cards when scouted. Element: `.go-scouted` strip `Potential A-–B+ · Athleticism C-–D · Technique C+–C- · IQ D–F`. Problem: "C-–D" and "C+–C-" are hard to parse (minus sign and range dash look alike); the same ranges are already shown below as "D to C-" cards, so the strip duplicates in a worse format. Heuristic/WCAG: H2, H8. Fix: use "to" as the cards do, or drop the strip on the profile and keep it only on the compact board card. SMALL.

**F14. Attribute labels in the player modal are truncated with no way to read them.**
Screen: player-week0, player-history (1280 and 400). Element: `.rating-label` `Intermediate Ac…`, `Throwing on th…`, `Pass Pro: Foot…`, `Decision Making` (clipped at 400). Problem: fixed 110px/96px label column. Heuristic/WCAG: H6; WCAG 1.4.10. Fix: let the label wrap (`white-space: normal`) or widen the column at the expense of the bar; add the full text as `title` as a fallback. SMALL.

**F15. "Hover a grade to see what it looks like in ratings" is hover-only.**
Screen: prospect-profile-scouted. Element: grade cards `ATHLETICISM D to C-` etc. with a hover tooltip (visible in prospect-profile-scouted-400-light as "Coachability C- to C+ looks like …"). Problem: tooltip content is unreachable on touch and by keyboard. Heuristic/WCAG: WCAG 1.4.13, 2.1.1. Fix: make each grade card a focusable `button` that toggles the explanation, or print the one-line "looks like" text under the grade. SMALL.

**F16. Trade modal: vague button, selectable options that will be refused, title mismatch.**
Screen: schedule-trade. Elements: button `Trade` (tiny, next to each `Trade for…` select); options such as `Miami · Power · you host · no: they want the home game`; modal title `Your non-conference games` opened from the button `Trade a non-conference game`. Problem: "Trade" does not say what happens; options the other school will refuse are selectable and only the suffix explains; the title does not match the entry point. Heuristic/WCAG: H2, H5, H4. Fix: label the button "Trade week 5 for this game"; `disabled` the refused options (or group them under "They would say no"); title the modal "Trade a non-conference game". SMALL.

**F17. Staff "Scouting area" picker does not look like a picker.**
Screen: staff (all widths). Element: native `<select>` showing `Texas (510)` with no chevron and no `go-sel` class. Problem: it renders as a static text box, so the one control on the Staff page that the player is meant to change looks read-only. Heuristic/WCAG: H4, H6. Fix: add the `go-sel` class (or the container rule) so it gets the house chevron. SMALL.

**F18. Class-year codes are run together: SRRS, FRRS, SORS, JRRS.**
Screens: depth, player pages, offseason-ad-meeting (`REDSHIRT → FRRS`), offseason-keep (`JRRS next year`). Problem: "redshirt senior" is encoded as a five-letter blob that a screen reader will spell and a newcomer cannot decode. Heuristic/WCAG: H2. Fix: "RS SR" / "RS FR" with a space, and spell out on the player page ("Redshirt freshman" already appears there). SMALL.

**F19. Portal-prospect "Transfer" tag is clipped at 400px.**
Screen: offseason-portal-prospects-400. Element: `.go-tag.muted` `TRANSFER` (82px of text in a 36px box). Problem: tag truncates in the status column on phones. Heuristic/WCAG: WCAG 1.4.10. Fix: let the tag column wrap or hide the TRANSFER tag on the portal list where every row is a transfer. SMALL.

**F20. "Let him go" on a departing player fires with no confirmation.**
Screen: offseason-keep. Element: `Let him go` button per card (`offseason.js:267`, `act(e.id, "release")`). Problem: the staff version of the same label confirms (`window.confirm("Let OC … go?")`, `offseason.js:423`); the player version does not and the card only changes to "letting go". The page copy says nobody decides until you advance, so this may be reversible, but the card gives no "take it back" affordance. Heuristic/WCAG: H3, H5. Fix: either a one-line confirm or an explicit "Undo" on the card while the stage is open. SMALL.

**F21. Recruiting side panel overflows its parent card.**
Screen: recruiting-board, recruiting-need-filter, recruiting-side-panel at 1280 (both themes). Element: `.go-board` side column; the `RECRUITING BOARD` card background ends at roughly the height of the card grid while the `SELECTED` panel continues 400px below it onto the page background. Problem: the panel visually floats outside its container. Heuristic/WCAG: H4, H8. Fix: `align-items: stretch` / let the card grow to the taller child. SMALL.

**F22. Rating bars convey a state by colour alone.**
Screen: player-week0, player-history. Element: `.rating-row` bars in red (Ty Hawkins: Top Speed, Agility, Leaping…) versus grey, and green (Bo Jackson 84+). Problem: red on a 55 and grey on a 54 encodes something (archetype-relevant? below-par?) that is never explained in text; the numbers are present so no data is lost, but the meaning of the colour is. Heuristic/WCAG: H2; WCAG 1.4.1 (information, not data). Fix: add the meaning to the "50 = average Power 4 starter" legend, e.g. "red: key to his archetype". SMALL.

**F23. "Sim to next season" sits beside every stage CTA with no confirmation visible.**
Screens: offseason-ad-meeting through offseason-staff. Element: secondary button `Sim to next season` next to `KEEP THEM HOME` / `OPEN THE TRANSFER PORTAL` / `STAFF CHANGES`. Problem: one mis-tap skips every remaining decision of the offseason; whether a confirm exists could not be verified from the captures. Heuristic/WCAG: H3, H5. Fix: confirm with a summary of what will be auto-decided ("9 players deciding, 11 portal spots, staff unchanged"), or disable until the player has visited each stage once. SMALL (verify first).

## Accessibility summary (WCAG 2.2 AA)

- Perceivable: text contrast is good in both themes on every screen inspected; UI-component contrast fails on the depth-chart controls (F1) and is borderline on the hire bar (F12) and dark-theme radios (F26). Information by colour alone on rating bars (F22). Reflow: three tables need horizontal scroll at 400 (F10), clipped label (F2), clipped tag (F19), truncated attribute names (F14).
- Operable: target size fails on depth controls (F1); inline action links at 20px (F8, F30); hover-only content (F1, F15). Keyboard: prospect cards handle Enter/Space; depth controls are real buttons; nothing observed that traps focus. Focus visibility not verifiable from captures.
- Understandable: reading level is good; jargon limited to class codes (F18). No unlabeled inputs reported by the automated check; selects carry `aria-label`s (`Team`, `Trade the week N game`, `Player to move`).
- Robust: `title`-only names on icon buttons (F1, F27); literal `null` in the DOM (F3).

## Positive findings — protect these

1. Explanatory copy. Every stage and panel opens with a short paragraph that explains the rule in plain English and names the cost ("NIL comes out of next year's budget, so it is money you will not have for recruits or the portal"). This is the best thing in the mode; keep it when tightening (F25).
2. Costs on the controls. `Message 5h`, `Call 15h`, `HC visit 30h`, `Offer 0h`, `Meet free` — the price is on the button, not in a tooltip.
3. Honest empty states: "YOUR BOARD IS EMPTY … only an offer lets him commit to you" with a primary "Find prospects"; "Offer prospects on your board and your hottest targets show here"; "No recruiting news yet. Commitments come over the fall." Each says what will appear and how to make it appear.
4. The schedule table's responsive column dropping (RANK and WHERE removed at 400) is the model for the other tables (F10).
5. Position changes apply instantly and list an `undo` per change; staff let-go asks a confirm that says what happens next. Replicate for player let-go (F20).
6. Pitch-strength tags (`STRONG` / `FAIR` / `WEAK`) next to each pitch line plus the "backfires" warning turn a hidden mechanic into a visible decision.
7. "Recommended for you" explains each recommendation in a sentence ("from a state you sign from · the star level you usually sign · a position you need: OL 2 left") and offers "Add these 50 to your board".
8. Both themes hold up: the red/green semantic pair, the kicker labels and the stat tiles stay legible in dark and light at both widths.
