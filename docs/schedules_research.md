# FBS Scheduling Research (post-2026 schedule generation)

Researched 2026-10-27 via web search/page fetches. "Announced" = conference/school release or major outlet; "Inferred" = my reading. Sources are cited per section.

## 1. Conference formats (2026) and announced changes

**SEC (16, no divisions).** 9 league games from 2026; 3 permanent opponents each, other 6 rotate so every pair meets at least every 2 years and home-and-away within 4. Permanent trios: Alabama–Auburn/Tennessee/Miss St; Arkansas–Missouri/Texas/LSU; Auburn–Georgia/Alabama/Vanderbilt; Florida–Georgia/S.Carolina/Kentucky; Georgia–Auburn/Florida/S.Carolina; Kentucky–S.Carolina/Tennessee/Florida; LSU–Arkansas/Ole Miss/Texas A&M; Miss St–Ole Miss/Alabama/Vanderbilt; Missouri–Arkansas/Texas A&M/Oklahoma; Oklahoma–Texas/Ole Miss/Missouri; Ole Miss–Miss St/LSU/Oklahoma; S.Carolina–Georgia/Kentucky/Florida; Tennessee–Alabama/Vanderbilt/Kentucky; Texas–Texas A&M/Arkansas/Oklahoma; Texas A&M–Texas/LSU/Missouri; Vanderbilt–Tennessee/Miss St/Auburn. Opponents announced 2026–29. Non-conference: 3 games, one vs ACC/Big Ten/Big 12/Notre Dame every year (since 2016). No published FCS cap; one FCS game per year is normal (inferred). SECCG Sat Dec 5, Atlanta. Sources: [FBS SEC 2026–29](https://fbschedules.com/?p=216624), [CBS](https://www.cbssports.com/college-football/news/sec-moves-closer-to-adopting-nine-game-conference-slate-as-cfp-metrics-shift-emphasis-on-schedule-strength), [FBS 2026 SEC](https://fbschedules.com/2026-sec-football-schedule-announced/).

**Big Ten (18, no divisions).** 9 games, "Flex Protect" 2024–28: 12 protected pairs (Illinois–Northwestern, Illinois–Purdue, Indiana–Purdue, Iowa–Minnesota, Iowa–Wisconsin, Iowa–Nebraska, Maryland–Rutgers, Michigan–Michigan St, Michigan–Ohio St, Minnesota–Wisconsin, Oregon–Washington, UCLA–USC); every other pair meets at least twice (one home, one away) and at most three times in five years; 5/4 home split alternates. Non-conference: 3 games; since 2016 one Power opponent required (BYU/ND count) and no FCS games, except (2017) one FCS game in a year with only 4 conference home games. CG Sat Dec 5, Indianapolis. Nothing announced for 2029+. Sources: [FootballScoop](https://footballscoop.com/news/big-ten-releases-schedules-including-new-members-usc-ucla-oregon-and-washington), [Huskers.com](https://huskers.com/news/2023/10/5/big-ten-unveils-future-football-opponents), [FBS B1G FCS policy](https://fbschedules.com/big-ten-loosens-restrictions-scheduling-fcs-teams/).

**ACC (17, no divisions).** Leaving the 2023–26 "3-5-5" 8-game model. 2026 bridge: 12 teams play 9, five (BC, Clemson, FSU, Georgia Tech, UNC) play 8. From 2027, 16 play 9 and one plays 8: Clemson 2027–28, Georgia Tech 2029 and 2031, Syracuse 2030, FSU 2032 (McMurphy report). 9-game teams need ≥1 Power non-conference opponent, 8-game teams ≥2 ("9+1 / 8+2", ≥10 Power games each). Notre Dame counts as a Power non-conference game. 2027 opponents published. Protected pods reported but unconfirmed against an ACC release: BC/Miami/Pitt/Syracuse, Cal/Louisville/SMU/Stanford, Duke/NC State/UNC/Wake Forest, Clemson/FSU/Georgia Tech, plus FSU–Virginia, Virginia Tech–Wake Forest. The 2024–30 model capped legacy schools at 3 California trips in 7 years, none consecutive. CG Sat Dec 5, Charlotte. Sources: [AP](https://www.whsv.com/2025/12/17/accs-shift-new-football-scheduling-model-will-have-12-17-teams-playing-9-league-games-2026/), [FBS 2027 opponents](https://fbschedules.com/acc-announces-2027-football-opponents/), [FBS 8-game teams](https://fbschedules.com/acc-finalizes-teams-playing-eight-league-games-from-2027-32-per-report/), [ESPN 2023 model](https://www.espn.com/college-football/story/_/id/38781798/acc-unveils-7-year-football-slate-new-17-team-league).

**Big 12 (16, no divisions).** 9 games, 5/4–4/5 alternation. 2024–27 matrix: four protected pairs (Baylor–TCU, Arizona–Arizona St, BYU–Utah, Kansas–Kansas St); every other pair meets home and away within four years, no same-site repeats in consecutive years, geography balanced. No 2028+ matrix announced yet. 3 non-conference games, no formal Power rule (13 Power/ND games in 2026); two 2026 European games. CG Fri Dec 4, 2026, Arlington (Fri/Sat rotation). Sources: [FBS Big 12 format](https://fbschedules.com/big-12-football-schedule-opponents-format-set-for-2024-through-2027-season/), [AP 2026](https://www.aol.com/articles/2026-big-12-football-schedule-173419043.html).

**Pac-12 (8 football: Oregon St, Washington St, Boise St, Colorado St, Fresno St, San Diego St, Utah St, Texas St; Gonzaga non-football).** 7-game round robin Weeks 5–12 with one bye, 4 non-conference games Weeks 1–4, Week 13 "flex" game between members (non-conference, set six days out for CFP positioning); ≥6 home games. CG Fri Dec 4 at No. 1 seed. A 9th football member (Memphis/Rice/UTSA floated) and 8-game options discussed for 2027–28 or later. Sources: [FBS Pac-12](https://fbschedules.com/2026-pac-12-football-schedule-announced/), [CBS](https://mauth.cbssports.com/college-football/news/pac-12-football-schedule-2026-flex-week-13/), [SI](https://www.si.com/college/san-diego-state/football/pac-12-commissioner-on-expansion-i-think-we-are-at-that-point).

**American (14, one division).** 8 games 4H/4A; no same-site repeat year to year; no opponent skipped two straight years; each team ≥1 road game in Eastern and Central zones and a Texas trip within 2 years. Army–Navy sits outside the league slate. CG Sat Dec 5 at higher seed. Source: [FBS American](https://fbschedules.com/american-football-schedule-format-opponents-set-for-2026-season/).

**Mountain West (10: Air Force, Hawaii, Nevada, New Mexico, San Jose St, UNLV, Wyoming, Northern Illinois, UTEP, North Dakota St).** 8 games 4H/4A (skip one opponent), 4 non-conference; Hawaii and its visitors may play a 13th game. Stays at 8 for now; Grand Canyon is non-football. CG Dec 4/5 at higher seed. Sources: [FBS MW](https://fbschedules.com/2026-mountain-west-football-schedule-announced/), [Gazette](https://gazette.com/2026/02/07/mountain-west-football-schedule-would-remain-at-8-games-if-north-dakota-state-joins-conference/).

**Sun Belt (14, East/West — only FBS league with divisions).** East: App State, Coastal Carolina, Georgia Southern, Georgia State, JMU, Marshall, Old Dominion; West: Arkansas St, Louisiana, Louisiana Tech, South Alabama, Southern Miss, Troy, ULM. 8 games = 6 division + 2 cross-division (1H/1A), every cross pair at least once in 4 years. CG Fri Dec 4 at the better division winner. Sources: [FBS Sun Belt](https://fbschedules.com/2026-sun-belt-football-schedule-announced-includes-louisiana-tech/), [Sun Belt release](https://sunbeltsports.org/news/2025/1/9/sun-belt-announces-2025-football-cross-divisional-opponents.aspx).

**MAC (13: +Sacramento St, −Northern Illinois).** No divisions; 8 games (miss 4) in 2026–27, 9 from 2028 (announced); midweek November games. CG Sat Dec 5, Ford Field. Sources: [FBS MAC](https://fbschedules.com/2026-mac-football-schedule-announced/), [ESPN](https://africa.espn.com/college-football/story/_/id/49949319/mac-moving-9-game-conference-schedule-2028-season).

**CUSA (10 after Louisiana Tech's exit: Delaware, FIU, Jacksonville St, Kennesaw St, Liberty, Middle Tennessee, Missouri St, New Mexico St, Sam Houston, WKU).** No divisions; 8 games 4H/4A; many Tue/Wed TV dates; CG Fri Dec 4 at higher seed. Sources: [CUSA](https://conferenceusa.com/news/2026/3/13/fb-cusa-announces-2026-football-schedule.aspx), [WBKO](https://www.wbko.com/2026/04/29/cusa-announces-revised-2026-football-schedule/).

**Independents: Notre Dame, UConn.** Notre Dame: 5 ACC games/year through 2037, Navy annually (rotating neutral/home sites), Stanford most years, USC unscheduled after 2026 (resumes 2030–31), rest are Power home-and-homes plus a buy game. Sources: [FBS Independents](https://fbschedules.com/2026-fbs-independents-football-helmet-schedule/), [FBS Notre Dame](https://fbschedules.com/ncaa/indep/notre-dame-fighting-irish.php), [SI](https://www.si.com/college-football/notre-dame-football-fills-future-schedule-signaling-end-to-historic-usc-series).

## 2. Non-conference patterns (mostly inferred from 2026 slates and the rules above)

- SEC/Big Ten/Big 12 (3 non-conf): 1 Power/ND (home-and-home or neutral kickoff), 1 G5 buy, 1 FCS buy; Big Ten usually swaps FCS for a second G5. ACC: same, with the 8-game team adding a second Power game.
- G5 (4 non-conf): 1–2 Power road paydays, 1 FCS home game, 1 G5 home-and-home; Pac-12 adds the flex game.
- Buy games are one-offs, never returned; 2026 FCS guarantees reached ~$740–775k (McNeese–LSU, FAMU–Miami), G5 higher (NIU got $1.4M from Notre Dame); ~55 guarantee games in Week 1. Sources: [FOS](https://frontofficesports.com/article/college-football-guarantee-games/), [McNeese–LSU](https://crescentcitysports.com/mcneese-to-play-lsu-in-football-for-record-guarantee-amount-in-2026-2029/).

## 4. Calendar rules

- 2026: Week 0 Sat Aug 29 (8 games; limited to Hawaii-related, international and FCS-opponent games), Week 1 Thu Sept 3–Mon Sept 7, regular season ends Sat Nov 28: 14 Saturdays for 12 games, so one bye (two for Week 0 teams); Hawaii exemption allows 13 games (SJSU 2026). From 2027 every FBS team may open in Week 0 under a standardized 14-week season (Week 0 Aug 28, 2027; Aug 26, 2028). Sources: [FBS Week 0](https://fbschedules.com/?p=229798), [Fox](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/college-football/when-does-2026-college-football-start-week-0-1-schedule-how-watch).
- Championship games: Fri Dec 4 (CUSA, Sun Belt, Big 12, Pac-12, MW) and Sat Dec 5 (ACC, MAC, SEC, American, Big Ten), i.e. the weekend after the last regular-season Saturday. Source: [FBS CG list](https://fbschedules.com/college-football-conference-championship-games).
- Thanksgiving week 2026: Thu TCU at Texas Tech; Black Friday Nebraska–Iowa, Texas at Texas A&M, Egg Bowl, Florida at Florida St, Minnesota–Wisconsin, West Virginia–Utah plus G5 games; Saturday: Iron Bowl, Michigan–Ohio St, USC–UCLA, Oregon–Washington, Georgia–Georgia Tech, Clemson–S.Carolina, Kentucky–Louisville, LSU–Arkansas, Oklahoma–Missouri, Purdue–Indiana. Source: [Fox Friday list](https://www.foxnews.com/sports/2026-college-football-friday-schedule-dates-times-tv-channels).
- Army–Navy: second Saturday of December, after CG weekend: Dec 12, 2026 (MetLife); Dec 11, 2027 (Philadelphia); Dec 9, 2028; Dec 8, 2029; Dec 14, 2030; Dec 13, 2031; Dec 11, 2032.
- CFP 2026–27: 12 teams (5 champions + 7 at-large); bracket Sun Dec 6; first round Dec 18–19 at campus sites; quarterfinals Dec 30 and Jan 1 (Fiesta, Cotton, Rose, Peach); semifinals Jan 14–15 (Orange, Sugar); final Mon Jan 25, 2027, Las Vegas; two-week QF–SF gap also planned for 2027–28. As of Oct 15, 2026 ESPN reports a 24-team format "closing in" for 2027 (P4 champions + top G6 + 19 at-large, NY6 bowls host QF/SF, may end CGs) — unconfirmed. Other bowls fill mid-December to Jan 1. ## 5. Rules a generator should follow

1. 12 games per team (13 with the Hawaii exemption) across 14 weeks (Week 0 to Thanksgiving Saturday); one bye (two if Week 0), none in Week 1, byes in Weeks 4–11, never consecutive.
2. League games: SEC/Big Ten/Big 12 9; ACC 9 with one rotating 8-game team; American/MW/Sun Belt/CUSA/MAC 8 (MAC 9 from 2028); Pac-12 7 + Week 13 flex. Home split 5/4 alternating yearly (4/4 in 8-game leagues).
3. Protected rivals every year (SEC trios, Big Ten 12 pairs, Big 12 four pairs, ACC pods, Sun Belt divisions), in Week 13/Black Friday when they are traditional finales; Army–Navy the Saturday after CG weekend.
4. Rotation fairness: every unprotected pair meets home and away within the cycle (SEC 4, Big Ten 5, Big 12 4, ACC ~7 years); no same-site repeat in consecutive seasons; no opponent skipped two straight years in 8-game leagues.
5. Non-conference: Power teams and ND need ≥1 Power/ND opponent (ACC 8-game team 2); ≤1 FCS game, Big Ten 0 unless only 4 league home games; G5 1–2 Power road buy games and ≤1 FCS; buy games are home for the payer and never returned; Power vs Power is home-and-home or neutral. Non-conference games in Weeks 0–4 plus at most one late slot (annual rivalry or a November FCS game).
6. Seed announced series and annual rivalries (Sections 2–3) before filling random games; book new home-and-homes 4–8 years out, returning within 1–4 years.
7. Home count 6–8 per year (7 typical Power, 6 for G5 selling two paydays); two-year home totals differ by ≤1; no more than 2 straight road games (3 for G5 paydays) or 3 straight home games; Eastern teams ≤2 Pacific trips a year.
8. Weeknights: MAC/CUSA/AAC/MW fill Tue–Fri windows Oct–Nov; Power leagues a few Fridays plus Labor Day Thu/Sun/Mon openers; one Thanksgiving game, ~a dozen on Black Friday.
9. Postseason: CGs Fri/Sat after Week 13 (top two records, divisions only in Sun Belt), CFP selection next day, first round two weeks later on campus, quarterfinals around Jan 1, semis mid-January, final late January; keep a switch for a 16/24-team bracket from 2027 that may drop CGs.
